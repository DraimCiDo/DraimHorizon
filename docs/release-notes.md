**DraimHorizon 0.40.9 — Hide ground vegetation**

- Hide grasses, ferns, sugar cane, bamboo, flowers, crops, vines and other ground vegetation on the 3D minimap and world map. Terrain, tree leaves and water surfaces remain visible.
- Commands such as /claims, /bal, /worth and /market are sent only on Horizon SMP (play.playhorizon.dev). In single player and on other servers the mod stays silent.
